Simulator-verified electronics

Describe it. It gets wired, coded, and proven to run before you see it.

CircuitMind turns a plain-English idea into an ESP32 circuit and firmware, then runs it in a simulator. If it fails, the exact error goes back to the AI and it tries again. You only ever see a design that passed.

In progress

Mechanical builds get a second simulator.

An arm can pass every electrical check and still not reach the cup. For servos, arms and grippers, the motor signals from the first simulation drive a physics model, and three more things get checked:

  • Does the arm actually reach where it was told to go?
  • Do the servos have the torque to get there under load?
  • Can the power supply handle every motor moving at once?
